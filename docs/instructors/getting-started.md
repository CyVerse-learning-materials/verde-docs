# Creating a Course in AI-VERDE

## Request a Course or Team

To start the process to create a course or team in AI-VERDE, please send an email to **ai-verde-support@cyverse.org**. There is no form to fill out; course and team setup is handled by email.

## What Happens Next

Once you send your request:

- **CyVerse staff** will create your course/team in AI-VERDE.
- After your course/team is created, it will appear in the **AI-VERDE dashboard**.
- **CyVerse staff** will contact you and the owner (if different) after the course/team is created.

## Adding Your Roster

Once your course/team is created, you can add your students, TAs, and co-instructors in either of two ways:

- **Add them yourself** in the **Members** tab of your course. See [Managing a Course](managing-a-course.md#managing-members).
- **Email your roster** to **ai-verde-support@cyverse.org** and CyVerse staff will add the members for you.
