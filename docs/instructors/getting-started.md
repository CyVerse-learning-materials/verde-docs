# Getting Started as an Instructor

## Request a Course or Team

To request a course or team in AI-VERDE, email **ai-verde-support@cyverse.org**. Use this template to describe your request:

```text
Subject: AI-VERDE course or team request: [course or team name]

Course or team name: [name and course code, if applicable]
Owner: [name and institutional email address]
Start date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
End date: [YYYY-MM-DD, or ongoing]
Expected enrollment: [number of students or team members]
Intended use: [assignments, research tasks, or other activities]
```

If any details are undecided, note that in your email. You can add your roster after the course or team is created, as described below.

## What Happens Next

Once you send your request:

- **CyVerse staff** will create your course/team in AI-VERDE.
- After your course/team is created, it will appear in the **AI-VERDE dashboard**.
- **CyVerse staff** will contact you and the owner (if different) after the course/team is created.

## Adding Your Roster

Once your course/team is created, you can add your students, TAs, and co-instructors in either of two ways:

- **Add them yourself** in the **Members** tab of your course. See [Managing a Course](managing-a-course.md#managing-members).
- **Email your roster** to **ai-verde-support@cyverse.org** and CyVerse staff will add the members for you.
